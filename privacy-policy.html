Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10-02-2026

RapidAid is an emergency assistance mobile application designed to help users quickly connect with hospitals and emergency services. Your privacy is important to us, and this Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

a) Personal Information

  • Mobile phone number (for login and OTP verification)
  • Name
  • Gender
  • Emergency contact number

b) Location Information

  • Real-time GPS location during an emergency request

c) Usage Information

  • Emergency request timestamps
  • Basic app usage related to emergency actions

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to:

  • Verify your identity using OTP
  • Share emergency details with hospitals and police
  • Help responders reach your location quickly
  • Improve app performance and reliability

We do not use your data for advertising or marketing.

3. Data Sharing

Your information may be shared only when necessary:

  • With hospitals and emergency responders during an emergency
  • With law enforcement authorities if required by law

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Data is stored securely using industry-standard practices
  • OTPs are temporary and automatically expire
  • Access to sensitive data is restricted

While we take strong measures to protect your data, no system is completely secure.

5. Permissions Used

  • Location permission (for emergency location sharing)
  • Internet access (to communicate with backend services)

Permissions are used strictly for core app functionality.

6. Children’s Privacy

RapidAid is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
